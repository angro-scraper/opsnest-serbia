# OpsNest Desktop 2.13.8

- A linked owner or team device now reads its effective access through its
  revocable central team session. It no longer needs the legacy billing token
  to receive Founder, trial, Business or Pro feature limits.
- Founder/Pro access is refreshed automatically after the workspace opens and
  can also be refreshed from **Packages and payment**.
- No billing token, password or payment credential is copied to a team device.

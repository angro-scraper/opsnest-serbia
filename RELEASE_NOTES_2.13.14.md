# OpsNest 2.13.14

## Serbia company setup

- Added the official offline **KD 2010** catalogue from the Republic
  Statistical Office of Serbia, with 996 searchable hierarchy entries.
- Serbian companies choose a concrete four-digit activity code instead of
  typing unverified data. Invalid or non-specific codes cannot be saved.
- The selected code proposes a sensible operating profile, while keeping the
  company owner in control of every invoice and tax setting.
- Added standardized choices for legal form and Serbian tax treatment.
- Added an operational Serbia control list in Company data and the Finance
  centre. It is guidance for the work process, not a legal or tax opinion;
  submission remains subject to licensed-accountant review.

## Quality gate

- All 24 critical workflow tests passed before packaging.

No hidden seat fees

Five or twenty people are already included.

Business includes up to five people and Pro includes up to twenty. You do not pay an additional fee for each person until you need a larger package. One owner invites teammates, sets roles and can remove access at any time.

Owner / AdministratorControls company profile, billing, members and settings.
Project managerWorks with projects, invoices, costs and reports.
AccountantWorks with VAT, documents and exports.
OperatorEnters costs, documents and payments without deletion rights.
How do I pay after the trial?

Inside OpsNest open Plans and billing, choose Starter, Business or Pro, and continue to PayPal. Your next billing date appears in the same window after successful payment.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. The Plans and billing window contains a link to PayPal automatic payments, where you can cancel future renewals. Local data remains available in read-only mode after paid access ends.

Does the trial require a card?

No. A seven-day Pro trial starts at company registration without a card. You choose whether to purchase when the trial is ending.

What if I need more than 20 people?

Contact support and we can review a workspace plan that fits your organization before anyone is charged.

Privacy notice

Your records stay practical, private and under your control.

OpsNest is designed around local Windows data. The service only receives the minimum information needed for registration, subscriptions and the support request you choose to send.

Local records by default

Invoices, customers, projects, attachments and accounting records are stored in your local OpsNest data folder. You decide where local backups are stored.

Optional team synchronization

Business and Pro can use secure central team accounts. When a company enables this feature, selected workspace changes are synchronized so invited members can work with the same company workspace. The owner controls members and permissions.

Registration and billing

For registration and subscription status, OpsNest Cloud processes the company name, business e-mail, workspace identifier, country, selected package and the minimum PayPal subscription identifiers needed to confirm access. PayPal processes payment credentials. OpsNest does not receive or store card details.

Support diagnostics

The optional diagnostic contains only the app version, operating system, license state and the note you write. It never includes invoices, PDF files, attachments, customer records, passwords, PINs or payment data.

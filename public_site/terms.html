Terms of use

Clear rules for a practical accounting workspace.

OpsNest helps companies organize project records. It is a software tool and does not replace professional accounting, tax or legal advice.

Trial and subscription

Every newly registered company receives seven days of Pro features without entering a payment card. When the trial ends, the company can select a monthly package through PayPal. Current limits and included features are displayed before purchase in the app and on the pricing page.

Company responsibility

You are responsible for reviewing document values, VAT treatment, invoice numbering, legal compliance and backups before issuing, filing or reporting documents. The app can assist with calculations and organization but cannot determine all country-specific obligations.

Team access

The company owner is responsible for inviting team members, assigning roles and removing access when a person no longer needs the workspace. Keep account credentials confidential.

Updates and availability

We may improve OpsNest, publish updates and adjust the product as it evolves. We will make reasonable efforts to keep core service functions available, but maintain local backups for business continuity.

Support

Contact support@opsnestone.com for product support. Do not send passwords, payment data or unredacted confidential documents by e-mail.
